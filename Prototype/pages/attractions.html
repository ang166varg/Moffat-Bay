Discover Adventure at Moffat Bay

Moffat Bay Lodge offers guests access to peaceful scenery and exciting outdoor activities. Whether you want a relaxing day on the water or an unforgettable wildlife experience, the island has something for everyone to enjoy.

Featured Activities

🥾 Hiking

Take in the beauty of the island through scenic hiking trails surrounded by forests, coastal views, and fresh open air. Hiking is a great way to explore the landscape and enjoy peaceful time in nature.

🛶 Kayaking

Paddle along calm waters and experience the shoreline from a new perspective. Kayaking allows guests to enjoy the bay up close while taking in the quiet and natural beauty of the island.

🐋 Whale Watching

Whale watching is one of the most memorable attractions at Moffat Bay. Guests may have the chance to spot whales and other marine wildlife while enjoying guided or self-planned waterfront experiences.

🤿 Scuba Diving

Discover the underwater world surrounding the island through scuba diving. This activity gives guests the opportunity to experience marine life, explore clear waters, and enjoy a more adventurous side of Moffat Bay.

Plan Your Island Adventure

Reserve your stay at Moffat Bay Lodge and enjoy hiking, kayaking, whale watching, and scuba diving during your visit.
